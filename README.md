# HelpByExperts

> $3 expert consultations — verified plumbers, electricians, mechanics, HVAC techs, lawyers & more.

## What is HelpByExperts?

[HelpByExperts](https://www.helpbyexperts.com) is an online consultation platform where users describe their problem, an AI assistant (Ava) handles intake and routes them to the right category, and a verified expert provides personalized advice via chat.

## How It Works

1. Describe your problem on [helpbyexperts.com](https://www.helpbyexperts.com)
2. Ava (AI assistant) gathers details and identifies the category
3. Pay $3 (one-time, no subscription)
4. A verified expert responds in under 15 minutes
5. 100% money-back guarantee
